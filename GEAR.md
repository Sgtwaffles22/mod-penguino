# Level 80 gear presets

These are fixed endgame presets for the original WotLK 3.3.5a item data.
PvE selections use heroic ICC, heroic Ruby Sanctum, tier 10, and relevant older best-in-slot items.
PvP selections use final-season Wrathful gear with appropriate raid offsets and weapons.
Individual best-in-slot choices depend on talents, gems, enchants, raid buffs, and arena composition.
The reward contains equipment; players select their own talents, gems, and enchants.

The linked Icy Veins guides are by Abide, Sellin, Seksixeny, Wrdlbrmpft, and Crix.
Item IDs and equip restrictions were checked against AzerothCore `item_template` data.
Early-season Mage PvP recommendations are upgraded to Wrathful equivalents.
Holy Priest uses the Discipline healer PvP preset; Fury uses Arms armor with a second two-handed weapon;
Blood/Frost use Unholy PvP armor; Combat and Assassination use Subtlety armor with appropriate weapons;
Demonology uses the Affliction arena set. Bear presets prioritize tank trinkets.
Subtlety uses daggers for Ambush/Backstab. Enhancement has both weapons, including its raid spellhance main hand.
Mislabeled Warlock shoulders/chest links were corrected to the corresponding tier 10 slots.

Faction variants of cloaks, Solace, and the PvP medallion are substituted when rewarding a player.
Humans receive the listed replacement trinket instead of a medallion because of Every Man for Himself.
Ashen Verdict ring presets grant the required exalted reputation.
